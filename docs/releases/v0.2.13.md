Inky 0.2.13 adds non-MPP Metal 3.2 simdgroup kernel support and M3 Ultra tunings (`--no-mpp`), expands multi-layer NVFP4/MXFP8 native checkpoint verification, and introduces the `tools/quality` card replay suite with M5 Pro `--kv8` and `reasoning_effort` benchmarks. These changes build on 0.2.12.

- **M3 Ultra tuning & non-MPP Metal 3.2 path (`t-later-m3`)**: Adds `tunings/m3-ultra-192gb.tuning` (`default_lanes = 64`, `kv_window_pct = 20`, `decode_splits = 16`, `use_mpp = false`) and `tunings/m3-ultra-512gb.tuning` (`default_lanes = 128`, `kv_window_pct = 15`, `decode_splits = 16`, `use_mpp = false`), guards Metal 4 MPP `tensor_ops` under `#ifndef NO_MPP` across `src/kernels.metal`, `src/prefill.metal`, and `src/bench_kernels.metal`, adds `Gpu::new_with_lang` (`Metal 3.2` when `!use_mpp`), `--no-mpp` / `INKY_DISABLE_MPP=1` overrides, `Engine::checked_serve_block_pool_counts_with_window_pct`, `Engine::split_target`, and non-MPP f32 decode logits (`encode_decode_logits_head`), plus `tests/m3_non_tensor_m5.rs` and multi-layer strict-W13 NVFP4+MXFP8 round-trip tests in `tools/inkconvert/formats_test.go`, `src/gguf.rs`, and `src/engine/tests.rs`.
- **Quality replay suite, `--kv8` parity, & effort sweep (`t-later-quality`, `t-later-kv8`, `t-later-effort`)**: Adds the zero-dependency Go quality replay suite in `tools/quality` (wired into `.github/workflows/ci.yml`), `tests/quality/cards.jsonl`, `tests/quality/results_m5_pro.json`, and `docs/QUALITY-REPLAY.md`, verifying unified diffs, patch application, Go AST symbols, and JSON tool calls across `fp16`, `--kv8`, and `reasoning_effort` (`none`/`low`/`medium`/`high`).
