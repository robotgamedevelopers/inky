Inky 0.2.11 adds speculative verification planning and Layer 3 paged rollback, shares deterministic `top_k` tie ordering between direct and speculative sampling, adds grouped shared-prefix tile traces and replay benchmarks, and completes the remaining engine, cache, and CLI step-status gates. These changes build on 0.2.10.

- `inky verify` (`src/scheduler/verify.rs` and `src/verify.rs`) bounds verification spans to the 128-row logit arena (`Engine::verify_row_capacity`), emits the full remaining reply budget across multi-token acceptances (`SpecEmitState`), drives pluggable draft producers (`DraftProducer` and `NgramDraftProducer`), checks Metal completion before accepting any row, and rolls back rejected drafts across both Layer 2 contiguous buffers and Layer 3 paged `Full`, `Window`, and recurrent `cs` state (including `n_keep = 0` full aborts and 64-token and 1,024-token boundaries).
- Direct sampling (`Sampler::sample_next`) and speculative verification (`Sampler::draft` and `Sampler::verify_round`) share identical `(logit_desc, token_id_asc)` candidate ordering (`checked_argmax`), verified across 10,000-draw histograms at an exact `top_k` tie alongside untied and zero-temperature controls.
- `scripts/check-cache-traces.sh` replays `blocks::walk_grouped_tiles` (`gl_4`) against `specs/GroupedLayout.tla` (`StagedOnce`, `VisitedKeys`, `OrderedPartials`, and `StepConforms`) and requires planted internal-tile partial-boundary and donor-window mutations to fail. `specs/SplitArena.tla` and its five configurations check the fixed-split partials arena in `grouped`, while `specs/run_tlc.zsh` and `tests/check-layout-coverage.sh` unify nightly execution under `TLA_NIGHTLY=1` (`PrefixTreeNightly` exhaustive plus explicit 21-node `PrefixTree` simulation) and verify POSIX `sh` execution without `zsh`.
- `inky sharedkv` verifies bitwise grouped-read parity across 9 attention boundaries (`63` through `1,025`) with `INK_SHAREDKV_SABOTAGE=1`, `inky prefixbench` records physical K/V pool bytes, peak Metal allocation, and end-to-end step latency across matched shared-prefix replays (`docs/SHARED-PREFIX.md`), `Session::run_chat_turn` rolls back failed early/final prefill chunks and greedy/sampled decode steps cleanly for the next CLI chat turn, and M5 model-backed tests verify single-token debug Q8 decoding, cached-prompt first-token LoopCD, and resident-versus-disk logits across the 32K staging resize transition (`32,756..=32,832`).
