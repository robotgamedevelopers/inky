Inky 0.2.6 hardens HTTP serving and recording, moves SSD cache restoration off the scheduler thread, and expands GPU, TLA+, and fault-injection gates. These changes build on 0.2.5.

- The server restricts `--bind` to loopback or private addresses, requires authentication on non-loopback binds unless explicitly overridden, and rejects browser `Origin`, cross-site `Sec-Fetch-Site`, non-loopback `Host`, and non-JSON `POST` headers on unauthenticated loopback listeners before reading any body.
- Streaming responses enforce a 15-second cumulative write-stall budget and a 600-second total stream ceiling so stalled clients release their lane. Accept loops back off and rate-limit logs on descriptor exhaustion, and startup raises and clamps the connection cap below `RLIMIT_NOFILE`.
- Startup refuses K/V budgets below one request's hard block floor. Prefix admission hashes 64-token blocks in linear time, span allocation checks capacity before evicting cached checkpoints and rolls back cleanly, and pool-deferred lanes wake as soon as checkpoints seal or deduplicate below the 1,024-token window.
- Cold SSD checkpoints restore and verify SHA-256 checksums on a background worker while ready lanes continue decoding, persist `spill-index.bin` across restarts, and qualify over HTTP on M5 across eviction, restart, early branching, and corrupt-block fallback.
- Fleet card recording is opt-in, the recorder bounds queued bytes to 64 MiB and repairs or rolls back torn JSONL tails, and refused requests allocate no turn or body copy. The memory helper keys saved limits on `kern.bootsessionuuid` across clock steps and checks startup traces against `MemMode.tla`.
- CI gates GPU LoopCD dry passes and blending, blocked-kernel batch invariance past 16 lanes and 1,024 positions, poisoned-slot stray-write detection, server-planted `servecheck` faults, deterministic fake-clock connection tests, and non-zero exit codes on argmax mismatches or sabotaged probes.
