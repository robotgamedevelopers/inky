Inky 0.2.15 unifies cache-fixed-split attention across contiguous, paged, and disk-backed prefill and decode, adds coalesced single-read expert-grouped MoE and projection kernels for decode-size batches, and adds an automated `perfgate` envelope check across 2K, 20K, and 42K contexts for 1 to 64 lanes. These changes build on 0.2.14 while preserving DSpark speculative serving (`--dspark`), non-MPP Metal 3.2 execution (`--no-mpp`), opt-in full-attention far-key pruning (`--full-attn-budget`), and sub-4-bit weight quantizations (`Q3_K`, `Q2_K`, `Q3_0`).

- Unified cache-fixed-split attention (64-token sliding-window splits and 256-token full-attention splits, `#pragma clang fp contract(off) reassociate(off)`, GPU `tau`, half-rounded query/key/value IO, and `split_scratch_slots(max_ctx) = max(256, ceil(max_ctx / 256) + 1)`) now drives contiguous prefill and decode (`k_attn_fixed_b`, `k_attn_fixed_combine`, `k_attn`), paged Layer 3 prefill and solo/grouped shared-KV decode (`k_attn_fixed_b_blk`, `k_attn_dec_solo`, `k_attn_grp_dec`), and disk-backed (`--kv-disk`) generation, with `--full-attn-budget` far-key pruning wired into the fixed-split prefill passes as well. `inky crosspath` verifies bit-identical finite logits and K/V + convolution state hashes for 17-row prefill versus 16-row prefill plus 1-row decode across contiguous, paged, and `paged_vs_contiguous` at all 12 split boundaries (`63..2049`) alongside `INK_CROSSPATH_SABOTAGE=1` planted mismatch detection.
- Decode-size batches (`1..=128` rows) route active `(token, expert_slot)` assignments through `k_moe_topk_route_dec` and execute `16 x 128` coalesced tensor-core tiles (`k_moe_dec_sg4`, `k_moe_dec_sg8`, `k_gemm_dec_sg4`, `k_gemm_dec_sg8`). Eight threads per weight row stage 128 contiguous quantized weights (`Q4_K`, `Q6_K`, `Q5_0`, `Q8_0`, falling back via `deq16` for `Q3_K`, `Q2_K`, `Q3_0`, `BF16`, `NV4`, `MX8`) in `8 KiB` of threadgroup memory (`4` threadgroups per GPU core) with 4x fewer barriers (`18` on `ffn_gate_up_exps`, `9` on `ffn_down_exps`), reading each touched expert once across all routed lanes while keeping per-token accumulation and `k_moe_comb_h` slot summation bit-identical (`0` bit difference) to `INK_NO_GRP_MOE=1` and prefill (`k_gemm_sg4`/`k_gemm_sg8`).
- `inky perfgate` (`--max-lanes N`) and `tests/nightly.sh` measure solo (`t = 1`) and batched (`1..=64` lanes) decode step latency and Metal GPU memory allocation at `2,048` (`2K`), `20,480` (`20K`), and `43,008` (`42K`) context against the `docs/DESIGN.md` lane table and per-chip bandwidth and working-set ceilings, paired with `INK_PERFGATE_SABOTAGE=1` and `INK_DB_AB=1` nightly verification.
